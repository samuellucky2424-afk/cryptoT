Frequently Asked Questions

Are my funds held in custody? expand_more

No. CryptoTransfer operates entirely non-custodially. We do not store or touch your private keys, relying on battle-tested bridging contracts to coordinate transfers.

What are the transfer fees? expand_more

Platform bridging fees range from 0.05% to 0.15% depending on liquidity route depth. Real-time gas fees are estimated upfront with no hidden spreads.

How fast are destination transfers? expand_more

Most transfers conclude in under 60 seconds. Speed depends primarily on the confirmation threshold required by destination exchanges like Binance or Coinbase.
